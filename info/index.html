What Are Zirconia Crowns?

A zirconia crown is a dental restoration that completely caps or encircles a tooth or a dental implant. Unlike other treatments, a crown works on a single tooth at a time, restoring its shape, strength and natural color.

Crowns for weakened teeth

Crowns support a tooth badly damaged by decay, too large for a regular filling, and protect a worn tooth from further damage.

Crowns for teeth at risk

Crowns hold a severely cracked or broken tooth together, and protect a tooth after a root canal.

Crowns over implants

If you already have an implant, a zirconia crown is the final aesthetic piece that lets you chew normally again.
